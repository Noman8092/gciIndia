Who we are

Global Computer Institute

GCI India (Global Computer Institute) is focused on providing practical computer education, digital skills and modern business-oriented training. We work with students who want a strong start in computing, professionals looking to add digital skills, and entrepreneurs who need practical support to run an online business.

Our programs are organised around the skills that today's digital economy actually asks for: computer literacy and applications, e-commerce operations, digital marketing, web development, and business support services such as compliance and registration.
